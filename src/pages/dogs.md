      Dogs are welcome to stay at Moonfleet by prior arrangement. There is an
      extra charge of £10 per dog, per night to allow for additional cleaning.


      Hot and cold water taps are available outside for muddy paws, and we keep a list of local dog-friendly places to eat. We ask dog owners to bring a dog bed, as dogs are not allowed on the furniture.


      Dogs are not allowed in the dining room during breakfast, but they are welcome in the bar in the evening.


      Moonfleet is close to Skinningrove Beach and Cattersty Sands, which makes it a lovely base for coastal walks and a paddle. Please check tide times and avoid cliff or beach routes when conditions are unsafe.


      Please contact us directly before booking so we can confirm your dog-friendly stay. You may also want to see [Room Rates](/room-rates/) and [Things To Do Near Moonfleet](/local-attractions/).
