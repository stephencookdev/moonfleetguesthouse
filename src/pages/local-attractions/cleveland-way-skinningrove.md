  - title: Is Skinningrove A Good Cleveland Way Stop?
    image: /assets/lulu-on-the-cliff.jpg
    imageAlt: Clifftop walk above Skinningrove on the Cleveland Way
    body: >-
      Yes, if you want a quieter overnight stop close to the coast path rather
      than a busy resort base. Skinningrove works well for walkers who prefer
      cliffs, beach, village history and a slower evening over a busier seafront
      town.


      The Cleveland Way is a 109 mile National Trail from Helmsley to Filey
      Brigg. On the coast it links Saltburn, Skinningrove, Staithes, Runswick
      Bay, Sandsend, Whitby and Robin Hood's Bay through some of the best-known
      North Yorkshire cliff scenery.
  - title: What Walkers Usually Need To Know
    body: >-
      Before choosing Skinningrove as a stop, think about the practical pieces
      that matter after a coastal walking day:


      * how close your accommodation is to the coast path

      * whether breakfast and packed lunch options are available

      * whether you need car parking for a point-to-point or circular plan

      * whether your route uses beach sections that depend on tide conditions

      * whether dogs are welcome, if you are walking with one


      Moonfleet is 5 minutes walk from the Cleveland Way and 300 yards from the
      sea, with breakfast included, packed lunches available upon request, free
      parking and dog-friendly stays by prior arrangement. See [Room
      Rates](/room-rates/) if that sounds like the kind of base you need.
  - title: Local Route Ideas From Skinningrove
    image: /assets/bkpam2100478_seacliffs_1.jpg
    imageAlt: Cattersty Sands beach and cliffs near Skinningrove
    body: >-
      From Skinningrove you can keep things gentle with Cattersty Sands,
      Skinningrove Beach, Land of Iron and the heritage trail, or make a fuller
      walking day towards Saltburn to the north or Staithes to the south. The
      surrounding coast is dramatic rather than manicured, which is a big part
      of its appeal.


      If you are deciding whether this stretch is for you, choose Skinningrove
      for quiet cliffs, local history and beach access; choose Saltburn, Staithes
      or Whitby for a busier day out.
  - title: Before You Set Out
    body: >-
      Check the official [National Trails Cleveland Way information](https://www.nationaltrail.co.uk/en_GB/trails/cleveland-way/)
      before planning a longer stage, and check tide and weather conditions on
      the day. Some beach and cliff routes are not sensible in poor weather or
      at the wrong state of tide.


      Walking with a dog? Check accommodation policies before you book, and be
      especially careful with tide, livestock and cliff-edge sections.
  - title: Where To Base Yourself
    body: >-
      Saltburn, Skinningrove and Staithes can all work as bases for this part of
      the Cleveland Way. Choose Saltburn for more facilities, Staithes for a
      classic fishing-village feel, or Skinningrove for a quieter stay between
      the two.


      If Skinningrove feels right, Moonfleet is a walker-friendly guest house
      close to the path and beach. You can [contact us](/contact-us/) to ask
      about direct booking, packed lunches, parking or dog-friendly rooms.
