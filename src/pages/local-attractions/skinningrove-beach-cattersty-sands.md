  - title: Is This The Right Beach For Your Stay?
    image: /assets/10078626-a170-4c4f-8473-ad4c82d4d043.jpg
    imageAlt: View over Cattersty Sands and the Skinningrove coastline near Moonfleet Guest House
    body: >-
      Skinningrove sits between Saltburn and Staithes, with Cattersty Sands and
      Skinningrove Beach giving this part of the coast its quieter feel. Expect
      open sand and shingle, cliffs, a historic jetty and a sense of space that
      feels very different from the larger resorts nearby.


      It suits visitors who want a quiet coastal base, cliff views, rockpool
      curiosity, birdwatching and slow days by the sea. If you want amusement
      arcades, lots of shops or a promenade feel, plan a day trip to Saltburn or
      Whitby instead.
  - title: Staying 300 Yards From The Sea
    body: >-
      If the beach is the reason you are considering Skinningrove, staying in
      the village makes the day easier. You can time walks around the tide,
      return for dry shoes or a rest, and keep the evening quieter than it would
      be in a larger resort.


      Moonfleet is 300 yards from the sea, with en-suite rooms, breakfast, free
      parking and direct booking. See [Room Rates](/room-rates/) or [Contact
      Moonfleet](/contact-us/) if you want a Skinningrove base close to the
      beach.
  - title: Easy Ways To Spend A Day Here
    body: >-
      A good Skinningrove day can be very simple: a beach walk when the tide is
      right, a visit to Land of Iron, then a short Cleveland Way or heritage
      trail loop before returning to the village.


      For a bigger day out, use Skinningrove as a quiet base and visit Saltburn,
      Staithes or Whitby without needing to sleep in the busier resort towns.
  - title: Tide And Cliff Safety
    body: >-
      The coast here changes quickly with tide, weather and cliff conditions.
      Check tide times before setting out, avoid cliff bases and cliff edges
      when conditions are unsafe, and follow official local beach safety
      guidance for the Saltburn to Skinningrove stretch.


      If in doubt, choose a village walk, the museum, or an inland route instead.
