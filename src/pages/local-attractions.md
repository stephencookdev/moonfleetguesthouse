  - body: >-
      Moonfleet is 300 yards from the sea and a short stroll from the quiet
      sands, cliffs and rockpools around Skinningrove Beach and Cattersty Sands.
      It is a good place for a slow beach wander, fossil hunting, birdwatching,
      or a dog-friendly coastal break by arrangement.


      Read our [Skinningrove Beach and Cattersty Sands guide](/local-attractions/skinningrove-beach-cattersty-sands/), and always check tide and cliff conditions before planning a beach route.

  - body: >-
      Moonfleet is minutes from the [Cleveland Way](https://www.nationaltrail.co.uk/en_GB/trails/cleveland-way/),
      the National Trail that follows the coast from Saltburn through
      Skinningrove, Staithes, Runswick Bay, Sandsend and Whitby. Walkers often
      use Skinningrove as a quieter coastal stopover between better-known
      resorts.


      See our [Cleveland Way walks from Skinningrove guide](/local-attractions/cleveland-way-skinningrove/) for stay notes, breakfast, packed lunches and booking links.
    image: /assets/bkpam2100478_seacliffs_1.jpg

  - body: >-
      Land of Iron tells the story of Skinningrove's ironstone mining and
      steelmaking heritage from the site of the old Loftus mine. It is one of
      the clearest ways to understand why this stretch of coast feels so
      distinctive: sea cliffs, village life and industrial history all close
      together.


      Read our [Land of Iron guide](/local-attractions/land-of-iron-skinningrove/) or visit [Land of Iron](https://landofiron.org.uk/) to plan tickets and opening times.
    image: /assets/land-of-iron-museum.jpeg

    title: Land of Iron Museum
  - body: >-
      The Skinningrove Heritage Trail explores the village's fishing,
      smuggling, ironstone and community history. It pairs naturally with Land
      of Iron, the beach and the Cleveland Way for guests who want a full local
      day without chasing a busy resort itinerary.


      Ask us for local pointers, and check the [National Trails Skinningrove Heritage Trail listing](https://www.nationaltrail.co.uk/en_GB/attraction/skinningrove-heritage-trail/) before setting out.

  - body: >-
      Cattersty Gill and Hummersea are nearby coastal nature reserves with
      wildflower-rich grassland, clifftop views and migrant bird interest. They
      are best treated as tide-aware, weather-aware walking ideas rather than
      fixed all-conditions routes.


      Read our [Cattersty Gill and Hummersea walks guide](/local-attractions/cattersty-gill-hummersea-walks/) for a gentle overview and official source links.
